Tony Eremin

I was born in Saint Petersburg, Russia, and now live in Prague as an immigrant. I have been photographing for many years, outside of any profession. My work is about memory and presence — the way a person can be felt through their absence, in the traces left on a place. I am drawn to nature and to how people live within it. Above all, I am interested in people as they are; if my approach has a name, it is humanist.
